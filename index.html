Explore & Build 3D

First-person 3D survival. Gather resources. Craft tools. Build machines. Research technology. Endless exploration awaits!

How to Play

1. Explore & Gather
Walk to trees and rocks. Look at them and press E or Click to harvest. You get wood, stone, food, and more. Resources respawn over time!
Forage Plants - Beware!
Look at plants and press E to pick them up - picking is always safe! Select a food item with 1-9 and press F to eat it. Some plants are nutritious, but some are poisonous or even deadly. You won't know which is which until you eat one!
2. Craft Tools
Press Q to open crafting. Make a wooden pickaxe (needs wood + stone) to mine harder rocks. Better tools = faster harvesting.
3. Build Structures
Press B to open build menu. Place a campfire to restore energy. Build furnaces to smelt ore into ingots.
4. Research Technology
Press T to open the tech tree. You earn Research Points (RP) from harvesting, crafting, and building labs. Unlock new tools, buildings, and automation!
5. Automate Everything
Build mining drills, power plants, assemblers, and solar panels. Create a self-running industrial empire!
Survival Tips
Walking slowly drains energy. Running (hold Shift) drains it faster. Swimming drains energy quickly - if you run out while swimming, you drown! Stay near a campfire to restore energy. If you die, you respawn nearby - no game over, ever!
